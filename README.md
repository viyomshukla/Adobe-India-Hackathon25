## 🚀 Challenge Descriptions

### 🧩 Round 1A: Understand Your Document
**Theme:** *Connecting the Dots Through Docs*

Your mission in this round is to **extract a structured outline** from a PDF document — mimicking how a machine would understand it.  
The expected output includes:
- Title  
- Headings (H1, H2, H3)  
- Organized in a clean, hierarchical format  

This structured understanding becomes the **foundation** for the next round.

➡️ For more information, refer to [`https://github.com/viyomshukla/Adobe-India-Hackathon25/blob/main/challenge_1a/Adobe_T1/README.md`](Challenge_1a/Adobe_T1/README.md)

---

### 🧠 Round 1B: Persona-Driven Document Intelligence
**Theme:** *“Connect What Matters — For the User Who Matters”*

This round focuses on building an **intelligent document analyst** system. The goal is to:
- Analyze a collection of documents
- Extract and **prioritize** the most relevant sections
- Tailor the results based on a **specific persona** and their **job-to-be-done**

➡️ For complete details, refer to [`Challenge_1b/adobe/README.md`](Challenge_1b/adobe/README.md)
